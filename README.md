# Edithunt

## The "Hunger Games" for Video Editors – May the best edit win!

---

## About The Project

Edithunt is our entrepreneurial class brainchild: an online service designed to revolutionize how video creators and editors connect. Forget endless portfolios and slow negotiations; we're bringing a competitive, fast-paced "bounty race" to the editing world.

Here's the gist:
*   **Creators** post a video editing request (a "bounty") with a deadline.
*   **Editors** ("hunters") jump in, submit their best work within the time limit.
*   Only **one** editor gets chosen by the creator and gets paid. The rest? They get invaluable portfolio pieces and bragging rights (maybe).

It's all about speed, skill, and a little bit of friendly competition to get creators the best possible edits, and editors the fastest path to building their reputation.

---

## Features

*   **Dual User Roles:** Separate experiences for Creators and Editors.
*   **Bounty Posting:** Creators can easily post new editing requests with details and deadlines.
*   **Bounty Board:** Editors can browse active bounties and see time remaining.
*   **Submission System:** Editors can submit their finished work for consideration.
*   **Winner Selection:** Creators choose their favorite edit, and the chosen editor gets the glory (and the payment!).
*   **Profile Pages:** Users can track their activity and stats.
*   **Sleek UI:** Designed with Tailwind CSS for a modern look.

## Developer's Notes & Demo Disclaimer

**⚠️ Presentation / Prototype Status ⚠️**

Please note that this repository is currently structured as a **presentation demo**. It serves as a visual and functional shell to demonstrate the concept and user flow of *Edithunt*.

*   **Not Production Ready:** Many backend processes (payment processing, complex video handling, email notifications) are simulated or omitted.
*   **"Empty Shell":** The database is intended to be populated with demo data for presentation purposes.
